The Art of Prompting

Every AI interaction starts with a prompt. The difference between generic output and exceptional results? How you write it. This module teaches you a repeatable framework for writing clear, structured prompts that work across any AI tool.

✕ Common Mistake
The Vague Prompt

Most people write prompts like text messages — short, vague, and hoping the AI reads their mind. It doesn't.

Weak Prompt
Write me a summary of this report
✓ Professional Result
The Layered Prompt

A well-crafted prompt gives the AI a complete brief. Every detail you add eliminates a decision the AI would otherwise guess on.

Strong Prompt
[Role] Act as a senior healthcare analyst. I have a 40-page quarterly report on member engagement. [Task] Summarize the top 5 findings in bullet points, prioritized by business impact. Use plain language suitable for a [Audience] C-suite audience. [Output Format] Keep it under 200 words.
