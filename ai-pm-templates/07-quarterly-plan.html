Quarterly Plan & Weekly Goals — [Team], [Quarter]

Why this template: Perplexity’s planning system (per co-founder Johnny Ho): quarterly objectives that are all measurable — “either quantifiable thresholds or Boolean” — a roadmap with a few large projects everyone knows about, and “75% weekly goals”: one top priority per week that the team aims to hit 75% of the time. OpenAI adds the frontier-lab caveat: set directional alignment but expect the plan to change as the technology moves — the planning is the point, not the plan.
