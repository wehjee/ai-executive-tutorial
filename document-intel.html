Module 02 — Document Creation

Documents, Done Faster with an Agent

Upload spreadsheets, reports, clinical data, or policy documents — and have AI analyze, summarize, and transform them

◆ Key Concept
What is "Context"?

When you upload a document to an AI, you're giving it context — the information it needs to reason about your specific situation. Unlike a Google search, AI doesn't just find answers. It reads your documents, understands relationships between data points, and generates new insights specific to your organization.

Think of it this way: a new hire on their first day can't answer questions about your business — they don't have context yet. But after reading your reports, memos, and data, they can start making connections. AI works the same way. The more relevant context you provide — a quarterly report, a policy document, a dataset — the sharper and more useful its output becomes.

Context is also what makes AI personal to your work. Two leaders can use the same AI tool, but by uploading different documents, they get entirely different — and relevant — results. Your context is your competitive advantage.

⚠ Guardrails
What to Keep in Mind
  • ! Verify all numbers and claims AI surfaces — it can misread complex tables
  • ! Be aware of PHI / HIPAA — do not upload protected health information to public AI tools
  • ! AI works best with structured data — clean spreadsheets yield better results than messy ones
  • ! Always review the output as the subject-matter expert — AI is the analyst, you are the editor-in-chief
✓ Prompt Templates
Ready-to-Use Prompts for Executives
Prompt Template
I've uploaded our Q3 operational report and financial summary. Please:

1. Identify the top 3 positive trends and top 3 areas of concern
2. Compare key metrics against Q2 performance
3. Create a 10-slide executive summary with:
   — One key insight per slide
   — Supporting data point for each
   — Recommended action items on the final slide
4. Use clear, non-technical language appropriate for a board audience
Prompt Template
Attached are our patient satisfaction scores, readmission rates, and staffing data for the past 12 months. Please:

1. Create a board-ready narrative that connects these three data sources
2. Highlight any correlations between staffing levels and outcomes
3. Structure as a presentation outline with executive summary, key findings, and strategic recommendations
4. Flag any data points that could raise board questions — and prepare suggested responses
Prompt Template
I've uploaded a spreadsheet with 18 months of departmental cost data. Please:

1. Identify the top 5 cost drivers and their growth trajectory
2. Flag any anomalies or unusual spikes
3. Compare our cost structure against typical healthcare benchmarks
4. Summarize in 3 bullet points I can share with my CFO today
